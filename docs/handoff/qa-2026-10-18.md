# Browser QA of the integrated demo, 2026-10-18

One pass over the 3-minute demo path of the pivoted app (commit e1ea886, "Integrate pivot: Findings -> Portfolio
overlap -> Protocol to terms -> Research"), in headless Chrome against the running app on the main checkout
(UI `http://localhost:5173`, API `http://localhost:8000`). The running servers were not restarted.

**Result.** Every step of the demo path works at 1440x900 and at 390x844. No crash, no view that fails to load,
no broken deep link, no console error, no page-level horizontal overflow. Every number checked on screen
(438 automated checks) matches a separate fetch of the same API request, so there is nothing to fix and **no
fix was made**. `npm run build` passes with zero TypeScript errors on this commit.

Findings that are not breakages are listed under "Forecast rule" and "Other notes". The main one: the overlap
view shows *estimated enrollment-window end* dates that fall after the registry snapshot.

    the viewport edge, and the scrollers, and take the screenshots.
  - `qa1018_measure_budget.js`: column widths of the budget table at 390 px (for the phone note below).
- Clear timing: the default Research clear (US-Northeast) took **96 s** from opening the Research tab.

## Forecast rule (Overlap, Protocol to terms, header, footer)

1. **Overlap: estimated enrollment-window end dates after the snapshot (borderline, not fixed).** Trial cards and
   trial rows show "Estimated window end" = start + 65% of (registered primary completion - start).
   - In the Regeneron preset, NCT05800015 shows **2027-10-15**, a year past the 2026-10-16 snapshot. It is
     followed by "enrollment closed by 2026-10-16, before the estimated window end".
   - At the snapshot date (the tab's default), 12 of AstraZeneca's 15 trials show window ends after the
     snapshot, up to 2028-07-13.
   - This is a projected end-of-enrollment date. It is labelled "estimated" and comes from registry dates, not
     from Concord's enrollment model. But it contradicts the view's own lede ("no dates after the registry
     snapshot") and its provenance note ("Later dates are refused: nothing is projected").
   - Suggested change (product decision, so not made here): show post-snapshot window ends as "after the
     snapshot".
2. Overlap "31.8 months both recruiting (estimated windows, cut at the snapshot)" is a past overlap duration,
   bounded by the snapshot. It is not a forecast.
3. Protocol to terms: no speedup, time-to-fill, rate or ratio anywhere on the page, in the markdown or in the
   print view. Only fee-schedule prices with provenance. "Objective response rate" appears only as a quoted
   registry outcome.
4. Header: only the brand line; clearing status shows only on Research. Footer: "Enrollment times appear only
   in the Research views and are model estimates." No claim.
5. Research shows in-sample ratios and speedups, labelled "In-sample model result (not a claim)", under the
   Research banner. Findings shows research ratios, labelled with how they were measured. Both are allowed.

## Other notes (not breakages, not fixed)

1. **Stale copy on Clear it.** The hint says "Clears take about 15–25 s", but the default clear took 96 s here
   (`ClearView.tsx`).
2. **Phone, Protocol to terms budget (step 4).**
   - The visit table is 443 px wide inside a 343 px scroller. The "Billed to insurance" column starts off-screen,
     and its header is cut at "BILLE" until you swipe. Nothing is hidden at page level.
   - The expanded per-line table has its own scroller (`.tf-lines-wrap`, 429 px), which is wider than the outer
     scroller and ends 92 px past it. So the Sponsor and Provenance columns of the lines need both scrollers
     moved. Everything is reachable.
   - The CSS comment's intent ("the nested line table scrolls on its own, so the visit totals stay in view on a
     phone") is only partly met. Fixing it would mean restyling the table, so it was left alone.
   - The compare item table (674 px in 343 px) scrolls the same way.
3. **Harness false positives at 390 px.** `layoutCheck` flags the timeline SVG's `<g>` elements: the `<svg>`
   root has UA `overflow: hidden`, but the SVG sits in `.tf-timeline-wrap` (`overflow-x: auto`), which fits the
   screen.
4. The Research sub-tab is labelled "Term sheet", not "Term sheet (old)" as the brief names it.
5. The Protocol to terms tab remembers the last trial in `localStorage` (`tf:nct`). A plain `#terms-flow` link
   can therefore open NCT06627647 after someone viewed it. The deep link `#terms-flow/NCT05800015` always opens
   the Regeneron trial.
