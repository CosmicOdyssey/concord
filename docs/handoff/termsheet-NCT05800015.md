## 6. Coverage rules and reviewer overrides

- **R1 (research)**, Investigational product and its administration: The investigational agent (or its blinded placebo), its preparation and the infusion that delivers it are paid by the sponsor.
- **R2 (research)**, Protocol-only test or activity: Items that exist only because of the protocol: PK and anti-drug-antibody samples, central confirmation, protocol ECGs, eligibility-only serology, questionnaires, central scan upload, protocol-timed safety and survival contacts.
- **R3 (routine)**, Standard of care at standard frequency: Visits, labs and imaging a patient on this therapy would receive anyway, at the standard-care frequency: billable to Medicare or the insurer under Medicare NCD 310.1 (routine costs in clinical trials).
- **R4 (research)**, More frequent than standard care: A routine item scheduled more often than the standard-care reference. Standard care's number of occurrences stays routine (R3); only the extra occurrences are research, and each node's reason names which ones (CT every 6 weeks against a 9-week reference: one scan in three).
- **R5 (routine)**, Standard-of-care comparator or backbone administration: Administration of approved standard therapy given in every arm is routine under Medicare NCD 310.1 (routine costs in clinical trials). Drug supplied free by the sponsor is never billed.

- Sponsor pays for all items labeled research; no double billing (NCD 310.1)
